What it does

A simple daily spending habit, designed around real-life personal and household expenses.

Personal and Family, without the confusion

Choose Personal or Family when recording a transaction. Switch views to understand each one separately—or see everything together. No shared accounts or bank connections are needed.

Set a budget, stay aware

Set limits separately for Personal and Family, and see how much is left to spend this month, with a clear warning when you go over.

Understand your spending

Spending by category, income against expenses, and how this month compares with last month or last year.

Cash, cards and payment sources

Label entries with your cards or cash and see what each one has spent, so you can check a minimum-spend target at a glance.

Receipts and tags

Attach a photo of the receipt, add tags, and tap a tag to see every entry that carries it.

Your records, your backup

Export records to Excel, CSV or PDF. Create a portable ZIP backup containing your transaction data and saved receipt photos, ready to restore on another device.

Also included: recurring expenses, credit-card payment reminders, quick recent-category suggestions, English and Chinese, SGD/MYR/USD entry, and optional PIN or biometric app lock.

Private by design, with no required account

Your expense entries and receipt photos are stored locally on your phone. You control when to share exports or backups. ByeLui uses a small Google AdMob banner, and the advertising SDK may process device and usage information as described in the privacy policy.

What it does

Built around one idea: your own money and your household’s money should not sit in the same list.

Personal and Family, kept apart

Every entry goes into either your Personal book or your Family book. Flip the switch to see only your own spending, or only the household’s. Budgets, analytics and exports follow the same switch, so a family grocery run never eats into your personal budget.

Monthly budgets

Set limits separately for Personal and Family, and see how much is left to spend this month, with a clear warning when you go over.

Analytics

Spending by category, income against expenses, and how this month compares with last month or last year.

Know which card you used

Label entries with your cards or cash and see what each one has spent, so you can check a minimum-spend target at a glance.

Receipts and tags

Attach a photo of the receipt, add tags, and tap a tag to see every entry that carries it.

Export and backup

Export to Excel, CSV or PDF with income and expense totals and a Personal or Family column, or save a backup file you control.

Also included: recurring bills, entries in any currency with totals in your own, and a PIN or fingerprint lock.

Your data stays on your phone

Entries, receipt photos and your PIN are stored on the device. ByeLui has no accounts and no servers of its own. The only third party is Google AdMob, which shows the banner ads.
